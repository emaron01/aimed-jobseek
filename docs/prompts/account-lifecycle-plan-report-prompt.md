PRODUCT OWNER DECISIONS
1. Full account wipe: one shared wipe operation deletes everything belonging to the account: every row owned by the user or organization, plus support tickets and their notes, transactional email events, and audit logs tied to them, plus the account's authentication records. Nothing personal remains in our systems. Mailbox connections are not used in this product; any mailbox connection rows are simply deleted with the account, with no provider revocation. Our database keeps no billing history; Stripe keeps its own transaction records and the Stripe Customer stays in Stripe. When Stripe is live, the wipe cancels any active subscription first so the seeker is never charged again.
2. Self-serve delete: the seeker has "Delete my account" in their settings, with a confirmation step. It runs the full wipe immediately and permanently, at any time, including while the account is read-only.
3. Cancellation and declined cards: service continues until the end of the paid period (no proration). When the paid period ends (the seeker cancelled, or a card was declined and not paid), the account becomes read-only for 30 days. This replaces today's immediate lock on cancellation and today's 14-day past-due grace followed by a lock.
4. Read-only: the seeker can see everything but cannot create or change anything, and no paid AI call can run for a read-only account (including queued jobs). During the 30 days they can pay or resubscribe, which restores full access with all their data. "Delete my account" still works.
5. Automatic wipe: after 30 days read-only with no payment, the account is automatically wiped with the same full wipe as decision 1, by a scheduled job.
6. Super Admin delete uses the same full wipe.
7. The inherited Aimed Outreach selective contact and outbound purge and its retention policy are replaced by this model.
