PRODUCT OWNER DECISIONS
1. Harper is an expert interview coach for ANY role in ANY industry and at any career stage. Nothing in Harper's prompts, rules, examples, or code may assume sales or any single profession. Sales examples used during development are examples only.
2. Methodology across ALL of Harper, enforced by structure, invisible to the seeker:
   a. Questions follow the WHO interview method (Geoff Smart, "Who: The A Method for Hiring"): every question Harper produces carries a required interview-type tag (for example screening, chronological walk-through, focused competency, reference-check prep). The tag drives how questions are chosen, grouped, and ordered. Harper cannot produce an untagged question.
   b. Every interview answer Harper produces is built in CAR parts (Challenge, Action, Result) by default, or STAR parts (Situation, Task, Action, Result) where setup matters, returned by the model as separate required fields. The server validates that every part is present and that the Result states an outcome; a missing part triggers the existing single bounded regeneration.
   c. The seeker never sees method names, tags, or part labels. Answers are composed from their parts into natural first-person answers for display on Harper and the Cheat Sheet.
   d. "All of Harper" means every place Harper produces interview questions or interview answers: gap questions and their polished answers, person prep, interviewer likely questions and sample answers (including cheat-sheet person guidance), the Where you stand topics (why this company, career walk-through), and the role-expertise questions in decision 4. Resume bullets are not interview answers and keep their current form.
3. Career-stage calibration from the Personal Profile, with no new seeker input: for people new to the workforce and college graduates, examples come from school, internships, projects, part-time work, and activities; for early through late career, from roles and results, matching the level of the job.
4. Harper's coaching set: at least 20 and at most 25 questions per application. Gap questions (from comparing the Personal Profile with the job) come first. Harper fills the rest with role-expertise coaching: the questions a hiring manager for this specific role and industry asks, WHO-style (including the chronological walk-through) plus the industry-standard competency questions for the role, each with an industry-standard answer built from the seeker's Personal Profile per decision 2, calibrated per decision 3. Harper decides the mix; if the gap analysis yields 5 questions, role-expertise coaching fills at least 15 more. Industry-standard questions are asked even when the profile is thin. Role-expertise questions render under Where you stand in the reserved role-expertise topic, and the seeker can reply to fill gaps through the existing reply, polish, and approve flow. Role-expertise generation is one paid run per application, cached, re-running only when the job changes, never on a page view. There is only one career walk-through per application across all sources.
5. Learnings default to the Hiring Manager: what the seeker learned (learned notes, stage notes before and after, and newly gained information) applies to the Hiring Manager's section by default and is background for other interviewers.
6. Learnings re-plan only on a real change: the reassess triggered by saving any of those notes runs only when their fingerprint changes (Phase 1 gate pattern), serialized, additive (never changes or deletes approved answers), never on a page view.
7. Remove the unused gap check: refreshConsultationOffer / detectInterviewNoteGap on saving notes after. If it makes a paid call and its output is no longer shown anywhere, plan its removal.
